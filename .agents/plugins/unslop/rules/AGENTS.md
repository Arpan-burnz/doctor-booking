# Unslop UI Design Principles

When designing, polishing, or refining user interfaces:
- Never settle for generic AI tropes (purple gradients, glowing cards, emoji labels, hype copy, or uneven spacing).
- Enforce quiet chrome: navigation, buttons, and surfaces use neutral ramps with one deliberate accent color.
- Put richness into moments of actual product content rather than spreading color and effects across backgrounds and borders.
- Follow the 9 redline passes: Spacing (4px grid), Color (tinted neutrals), Icons (consistent stroke), Copy (plain, no hype), Typography (sentence case, tabular numbers), Layout (real proportions), Details (nested radii), Visuals (real product context), and Motion (purposeful, no looping pulse).
